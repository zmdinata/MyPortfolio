# Design System

## Overview

Zacky Muhammad Dinata's portfolio is a dark, futuristic personal portfolio for an aspiring AI Engineer, data scientist, blockchain enthusiast, and NFT artist. The layout uses a fixed glass navigation bar, neon accents, rounded project cards, large section headings, and timeline-style experience entries. The visual identity is technical but personal: deep black surfaces, luminous cyan and purple accents, soft radial glows, and high-contrast headings. It should feel focused, modern, and career-ready, with enough color movement to signal AI, data, and Web3.

## Colors

- **Primary Surface**: `#06060E` - main page background.
- **Card Surface**: `#12121F` - dark panels and project cards.
- **Raised Surface**: `#161625` - secondary panels and controls.
- **Primary Text**: `#EAEAF2` - headings and key labels.
- **Muted Text**: `#8B8BA8` - paragraph copy and supporting metadata.
- **Purple Accent**: `#7C6FFF` - active nav pills, timeline dots, and emphasis.
- **Cyan Accent**: `#00D2FF` - brand mark and link highlights.
- **Mint Accent**: `#00DDB3` - underline details, arrows, and glow.
- **Hire Green**: `#22C55E` - availability badge.
- **Warm Highlight**: `#FF6B9D` - occasional glow contrast.

## Typography

- **Heading Font**: Outfit (300-900). Used for the brand wordmark, hero title, section titles, and card headings. Heavy weights carry the portfolio identity.
- **Body Font**: Inter (300-800). Used for paragraphs, metadata, navigation, and UI labels.
- **Fallback Accent**: Arial (600). Present in captured tokens but should stay fallback-only.
- **Hierarchy**: hero title around 64px, section titles around 40px, body copy 18-20px in video, labels 16px minimum.

## Elevation

Depth comes from translucent dark panels, 1px borders in purple/cyan-tinted low opacity, rounded corners, soft radial glows, and slight glass effects. Project cards and experience panels feel lifted through contrast and glow rather than heavy shadows. Background depth is created with teal and purple radial fields, thin circuit lines, timeline dots, and subtle moving particles.

## Components

- **Glass Navigation Rail**: fixed top bar with `ZMDINATA`, nav pills, theme toggle, and language chip.
- **Availability Badge**: green pill with dot, compact and high-signal.
- **Hero AI Circuit Field**: animated SVG-like circuit roots and floating AI core shapes behind the hero.
- **Neon Section Heading**: large Outfit heading with short cyan-to-purple underline.
- **Project Gallery Cards**: three cards showing data strategy, NFT art, and web programming work.
- **Experience Timeline**: vertical timeline with glowing dots and large dark information panels.
- **Skill Cards**: four rounded cards with centered white icons and concise skill groups.
- **Contact Button Row**: rounded outline buttons for Instagram, LinkedIn, GitHub, and Email.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use `#06060E` as the dominant canvas with localized cyan, purple, mint, and pink glow.
- Keep large headings crisp in Outfit and supporting copy restrained in Inter.
- Use captured screenshots generously as moving panels, especially projects, experience, and skills.
- Build motion around smooth parallax, scanning lines, orbiting cores, and timeline reveals.
- Keep borders subtle and luminous instead of using heavy shadows.

### Don'ts

- Do not turn the portfolio into a light theme or generic corporate deck.
- Do not use beige, flat gray, or one-note purple as the entire palette.
- Do not make text-only beats; the website's cards and screenshots are part of the identity.
- Do not use hard square cards; the captured UI uses rounded panels.
- Do not edit or recreate the Agent-Z mascot files from the original app.
